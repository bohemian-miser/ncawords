Dynamic Kernel NCA — Physics Interpolation & Organism Comparison

In our Dynamic Layer-Modulated Kernel NCA architecture, every cell dynamically synthesizes its 5×5 spatial perception filters from a basis modulated by its surrounding state. This demo lets you compare these models side-by-side on a shared 16-channel grid using the interpolation trick: a painted control field $B(x, y)$ smoothly interpolates between Model A and Model B cell-by-cell ($dx = (1-B) dx_A + B dx_B$).

How It Works

Standard Neural Cellular Automata use fixed $3\times3$ Sobel filters for spatial perception. In contrast, our Dynamic Layer-Modulated Kernel NCA evaluates a dynamic mixture of basis filters (directional derivatives, Laplacian rings, and center-surround diffusions) at each cell: 

αm(y, x) = Softmax( Conv2d3×3(state, Wsurround) + Wx x̄ + Wh h̄ )
Keff(y, x) = ∑m αm(y, x) · Basism

This allows the organism to employ completely different spatial filters along its outer perimeter (boundary preservation and edge detection), deep inside its body (stabilizing diffusion), and across empty space.

The Spatial Interpolation Trick

Because both models operate on identical 16-channel representations ($C=16, HN=128$), their proposed updates $dx_A$ and $dx_B$ exist in the same vector space. A scalar control field $B(x, y) \in [0, 1]$ smoothly interpolates between them: 

dx(x, y) = (1 - B(x, y)) · dxA(x, y) + B(x, y) · dxB(x, y)
x(x, y) ← x(x, y) + fire · dx(x, y)

Spontaneous Nucleation from Pure Noise

The new emoji models were trained on GPU from pure random noise rather than an artificial single seed pixel. Clicking Fill Pure Noise fills the grid with chaotic random values. Over the course of 50–100 steps, you will observe the left side spontaneously condense into Model A (e.g. Fire) while the right side condenses into Model B (e.g. Rocket), with an active morphological battle and chimera frontier along the interpolation boundary!

Model weights are read directly from the static deployment in runs.json. Inspect all trained models and timelapses in the Run Gallery.
